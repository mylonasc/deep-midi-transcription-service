# Open-Fret inference and duration utilities

Source: https://github.com/mylonasc/open-fret
Revision: `3a62b4cb06ff2900dc1d494b98fb5a3acb174f9f`.

`transc_service/tablature/durations.py` adapts the exact-duration decomposition
from upstream `open_fret/render.py`. `tools/install_open_fret.py` installs a
checksum-verified, inference-only subset of that revision, including its original
LICENSE. The license below applies to those utilities, not to model weights,
training data or underlying musical works. Upstream explicitly flags unresolved
dataset/model redistribution rights; public availability is not a rights grant.
